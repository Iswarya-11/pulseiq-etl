Attendance & Leave Dashboard — Design Notes

Dashboard layout
- Monthly Attendance Rate Trend: shows attendance changes over time.
- Department-wise Attendance Rate: compares attendance across departments.
- Weekday-wise Attendance Rate: highlights differences across weekdays.
- Approved Leave Days by Type: shows the distribution of approved leave.
- Monthly Late Arrivals Trend: tracks late-arrival counts over time.
- Monthly AI-Flagged Attendance Anomalies: highlights records flagged for review.

Visual style
- Navy: #1F3864
- Blue: #2E75B6
- Orange: #C55A11
- Green: #548235
- Grey: #7F7F7F

Formatting and usability
- Use clear chart titles and concise takeaways.
- Display percentages to one decimal place.
- Use chronological order for monthly charts.
- Avoid 3D charts and crowded pie charts.
- Label AI anomaly charts clearly as flagged records, not confirmed violations.
- Keep filters consistent and ensure date filters work as expected.

Limitations
- Sample data may not reflect real workforce behaviour.
- Raw counts can be affected by employee numbers and scheduled working days.
- A single year's seasonal pattern should not be treated as conclusive.
